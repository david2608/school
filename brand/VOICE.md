# Pedanyan Brand Voice

Pedanyan sounds like an experienced designer, teacher, and maker who still pays attention.

The working balance is:

- 75% editorial
- 20% skeptical or provocative
- 5% natural and conversational

## Character

Pedanyan is culturally curious, visually aware, and skeptical of hype. The voice is direct without becoming crude, intelligent without performing intelligence, and confident without repeating its credentials. It becomes warmer when inviting someone to take part. Dry or self-aware humor is welcome when it clarifies the point.

Pedanyan is not a motivational speaker, a corporate education company, or an agency trying to look rebellious.

## Editorial base

Lead with an observation or idea. Use clean rhythm, short paragraphs, concrete language, and enough space for the reader to think. Refer to photography, architecture, music, art, cities, culture, objects, or technology only when the connection helps explain the subject.

Do not confuse editorial writing with writing quotable sentences. A few lines may be memorable. Most sentences should make the argument clearly. Avoid turning every section into a contrast, slogan, rhetorical question, or dramatic one-line statement.

Example:

> Tools have changed several times since I started designing. The difficult questions have changed much less.

## Controlled provocation

Use no more than one strong provocation in a major section. It must express a defensible point of view and lead to something useful.

Example:

> AI can make a screen in seconds. It still cannot decide which screen should exist.

Do not manufacture controversy, attack competitors, or make every heading perform.

## Natural layer

Write closer to speech than advertising. Questions, ordinary observations, concrete examples, direct invitations, and occasional self-irony are useful. A strong pattern is idea, example, point.

English must be fluent. Preserve the directness and rhythm of Davit's Armenian LinkedIn writing, not its grammar or literal constructions.

## Credibility

Prefer numbers and evidence to adjectives and self-praise. Use only claims listed in [FACTS.md](FACTS.md) or verified in a named project source. Do not write a heroic biography.

## Registers

### Marketing and editorial

Use the full voice. Lead with a specific idea and allow a controlled provocation when it earns its place.

### Course and teaching

Be direct, explanatory, and practitioner-led. Tell students what to notice, do, test, or explain.

### Product UI

Keep the voice quiet. Clarity wins over personality.

### Long-form and thought leadership

Explore questions, observations, examples, counterpoints, and cultural connections. Avoid artificial profundity.

## Two tests

Genericness test: Could the exact paragraph work after replacing Pedanyan with another school? If yes, add a specific method, fact, example, observation, or point of view.

Idea test: Does the sentence contain an idea, or only tone? Remove or rewrite sentences that only sound like something.
