# Anti-Fluff Checklist

Flag these phrases unless a quoted source requires them:

- unlock your potential
- elevate your skills
- take your skills to the next level
- transformative journey
- embark on a journey
- cutting-edge
- empowering
- future-ready
- ever-evolving landscape
- unleash creativity
- master the art of
- creativity meets innovation
- designed for tomorrow
- thrive
- dynamic world
- more than just a course
- not just X, but Y
- whether you're a beginner or...
- real-world experience, unless the work is explained concretely

## Writing habits to flag

- excessive em dashes
- artificial three-item lists
- repeated contrast formulas
- excessive one-sentence paragraphs
- unnecessary rhetorical questions
- vague inspirational conclusions
- the same idea stated several ways
- meaningless adjectives
- generic startup language
- fake urgency
- excessive "we believe"
- a clever line where normal information would work better
- passive voice that hides the person doing the work
- inanimate concepts made to sound human

## Repair sequence

1. Remove the phrase.
2. Name the concrete action, example, method, or evidence.
3. Make the actor clear.
4. Keep the shortest version that retains the idea.

Do not replace one slogan with another.
