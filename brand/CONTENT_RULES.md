# Pedanyan Content Rules

Don't make normal information wear a brand costume.

## Priority

1. Specific over impressive
2. Clear over clever
3. Idea over tone
4. Evidence over adjective
5. Human over polished
6. Short over repetitive

## Product UI

For product UI, clarity wins over personality. State the action or status.

Use:

- Apply
- Open lesson
- Submit assignment
- View feedback
- Continue
- Assignment submitted.
- Feedback pending.
- Next lesson: User Research.

Avoid:

- Start your journey
- Unlock lesson
- Continue growing
- Begin transformation
- Your exciting learning journey continues!

Headlines may carry voice. Utility text must carry information.

## Marketing

- Decide the one idea before drafting.
- Use one strong provocation at most in a major section.
- Support claims with [FACTS.md](FACTS.md).
- Explain what students will do instead of promising vague transformation.
- Pass the genericness and idea tests in [VOICE.md](VOICE.md).
- Run [ANTI_FLUFF.md](ANTI_FLUFF.md) before delivery.

## Teaching

- Give the learner a clear action, distinction, or question.
- Use examples from practice.
- Explain terms without decorating them.
- Do not confuse confidence with certainty. Name trade-offs when they matter.

## Long-form

- Build an argument from observations and examples.
- Use cultural references only when they explain the idea.
- Allow questions and counterpoints.
- End when the point is made.

## Facts

Never silently invent a fact. When evidence is missing, remove the claim, write around it, or ask for confirmation.
